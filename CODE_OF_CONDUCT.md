# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Tag to be a respectful, focused, and welcoming project. Contributors are expected to treat each other with patience and care, especially when discussing privacy, reliability, accessibility, and product tradeoffs.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Protect private user data in issues, pull requests, screenshots, and logs.
- Prefer product decisions that make users feel more in control of their saved context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Uploading unredacted private screenshots, credentials, local databases, or sensitive logs.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions away from the project goals.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also limit participation for repeated or serious violations.
